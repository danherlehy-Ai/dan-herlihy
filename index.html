How he thinks

The Dan Herlehy operating system.

01

Don't guess.

Research it. Cross-check it. Cite the source. A slow accurate answer beats a fast unreliable one — every time.

02

Good / Better / Best.

Every decision gets three options, ranked. No analysis paralysis — just pick the tier the ROI justifies.

03

Automate the repetitive.

AI handles 80–95% of the grind; humans review the exceptions. If he's doing it twice, he's scripting it.

04

Four questions.

Can it make money? Can it scale? Can it be automated? Is the ROI worth it? A "no" anywhere kills the idea fast.
